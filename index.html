Our TikTok Publishing Tool

We use a small publishing tool, built on TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, to send our own finished videos to the Jofoo AI TikTok account.

  1. The account owner signs in with TikTok and approves access on TikTok's own consent screen.
  2. The owner chooses one finished video from this website's public media folder.
  3. The tool uploads that video to the TikTok inbox as a draft. It never posts publicly on its own.
  4. The owner opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, writes the caption, adds any AI-content label and taps Post.

The tool asks only for basic profile information (user.info.basic) and permission to upload drafts (video.upload). It is used only with the Jofoo AI account and does not read other people's data. Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Manage app permissions. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
