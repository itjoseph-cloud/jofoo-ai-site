<TLDR>Remove private details from your notes, paste them into ChatGPT with the prompt below, and ask for each task, who owns it and when it is due. Tell ChatGPT to write "Not stated" instead of guessing. Then check the list against your notes.</TLDR>

<Problem>The meeting is over, and your notes are a mess. They are half-sentences, first names and question marks. You are not sure who agreed to do what, or by when. You need a clear list to send out.</Problem>

<Why>
Meeting notes are written fast. Tasks get buried between ideas and side comments.

A chat tool can sort text that you paste in. But it works best when you say clearly what you want. OpenAI advises you to be clear and specific, and to give enough context.

It can also get things wrong. OpenAI says ChatGPT can give incorrect or misleading answers, and that you should use it as a first draft, not a final source.
</Why>

<Fix>Give ChatGPT your notes and one clear prompt. Ask for tasks, owners and dates, and tell it not to guess. Then check the list against your notes.</Fix>

<StepByStep>
<Step>**Make a safe copy of your notes.** Open your notes in a separate document. Remove names, phone numbers, email addresses, account numbers and company details. Replace them with placeholders such as Person A and Client X. You can put the real names back yourself at the end.</Step>
<Step>**Open ChatGPT and start a new chat.** A new chat gives this task its own space, so older messages do not fill it up.</Step>
<Step>**Paste the prompt.** Copy the first prompt below into the message box. Then paste your safe notes where it says to.</Step>
<Step>**Send it and read the list.** Check each action item against your notes. Look for three things: a task that is missing, an owner that was made up, and a date that was made up. Where ChatGPT wrote "Not stated", decide who and when yourself.</Step>
<Step>**Ask for changes.** If the first answer is not right, say what to change. OpenAI says good prompting is often a back-and-forth. You review the answer, then refine the prompt. For example, ask for a shorter list or a friendlier tone. The third prompt below asks ChatGPT to check for missed items.</Step>
<Step>**Copy the final list into your own document.** Put the real names back and add the dates you decided. Send it to your team only after you have read it once more.</Step>
</StepByStep>

<Prompt label="Notes to Action Items">Below are my meeting notes. Turn them into a list of action items. For each item, give the task, the person responsible and the due date. Use only what is in the notes. If the person or the date is not stated, write "Not stated". Do not guess. After the list, add a short list of decisions that were made and a short list of open questions. Notes: [paste your notes here]</Prompt>

<Prompt label="Practice Note (Made Up)">tues mtg w/ team. website redo, Sam says needs new photos by end of month?? Priya to ask printer about a quote. budget still not decided, maybe talk again next wk. Lee send out the survey, i think friday. parking?? nobody knows</Prompt>

<Prompt label="Check for Missed Items">Compare your list to my notes. Tell me if any action item in my notes is missing from your list. Tell me if you added anything that is not in my notes.</Prompt>

<Prompt label="Turn the List Into an Email">Turn the action list into a short, friendly email to the team. Keep every task, person and date exactly as listed. Keep "Not stated" where it appears.</Prompt>

<Tip>Not sure where to start? Try the made-up practice note first. It is sample data, not a real person's information. Names in your own notes should become placeholders before you paste them.</Tip>

<Warning>ChatGPT can drop an item or get one wrong. OpenAI says to always verify data and quotes. Read the list against your notes before you send it to anyone.</Warning>

<Safety>
Meeting notes often hold names, client details and numbers. Remove them before you paste, and keep passwords, account numbers and anything confidential out of the chat.

If your notes are about a client, a case, a patient or a private matter, do not paste them. Write the action list by hand instead.

## If It Does Not Work

- **The list leaves things out.** Send the third prompt, "Check for Missed Items". Then read your notes line by line yourself.
- **ChatGPT made up a person or a date.** Reply with "Use only what is in my notes. Write Not stated where the notes do not say." Then ask for the list again.
- **ChatGPT says the chat is full.** OpenAI says this is about how much ChatGPT can use in one conversation. You can start a new chat right away on the free plan. Paste only the part of your notes you want to work on.
