<TLDR>First check the OpenAI status page to see if ChatGPT is down. If it is not, check your internet, refresh or restart, update the app, and clear cookies as a last step.</TLDR>

<Problem>You open ChatGPT and nothing happens. The page stays blank, the app keeps loading, or you see an error. You are not sure if the problem is ChatGPT, your phone, or something you did.</Problem>

<Why>
Sometimes ChatGPT itself has a problem, and nothing you do will fix it until OpenAI does.

More often, the cause is close to home. A weak connection, an old version of the app, or saved website data can stop it from loading. On a work or school network, the network itself may block ChatGPT.
</Why>

<Fix>Work through five checks in order, easiest first. Stop as soon as ChatGPT works again.</Fix>

<StepByStep>
<Step>**Check if ChatGPT is down.** Open a web browser and go to status.openai.com. If it says "We're fully operational", OpenAI knows of no problem. If it shows a problem with ChatGPT, wait and try again later.</Step>
<Step>**Check your internet.** Open any other website or app. If nothing loads, the problem is your connection. On a phone, try switching between Wi-Fi and mobile data. On a work or school network, ChatGPT may be blocked, so try another network or ask your IT team.</Step>
<Step>**Refresh or restart.** On a computer, reload the page. On a phone, close the ChatGPT app and open it again. If a chat still will not open, OpenAI suggests opening a private browser window, signing in to the same account, and trying there.</Step>
<Step>**Update the app.** On iPhone: open the App Store, tap your photo at the top, tap App Updates, then tap Update next to ChatGPT. On Android: open Google Play, tap your profile icon at the top right, tap Manage apps & device, tap See details under "Updates available", then tap Update next to ChatGPT.</Step>
<Step>**Clear cookies and website data.** Do this last, because it can sign you out of websites. In Chrome on a computer: select More (the three dots, top right), then Delete browsing data. Choose a time range, select Cookies and other site data, and delete. On iPhone with Safari: go to Settings, tap Apps, select Safari, then tap Clear History and Website Data.</Step>
</StepByStep>
