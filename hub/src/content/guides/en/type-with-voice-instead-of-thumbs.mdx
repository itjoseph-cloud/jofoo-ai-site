<TLDR>On Android, tap a text field, tap the microphone icon on the Gboard keyboard, and speak when you see "Speak now". On iPhone, turn on Dictation in Settings, tap a text field, and tap the Dictate button. Say punctuation aloud, and know that voice typing does not work in every language.</TLDR>

<Problem>Typing a long message on a small screen is slow and tiring for your thumbs. You would rather just say it.</Problem>

<Why>
Both Android and iPhone can turn your speech into text. Google's Gboard keyboard does it on Android, and Google lists the same steps for Gboard on iPhone. Apple has Dictation built into the iPhone keyboard.
{/* CHECK: re-read on live page (Gboard S1 and S2 read as relays; Apple S3 is a search relay only) */}

Voice typing has limits. It does not work with every language, so check yours first.
</Why>

<Fix>Pick your phone below. Each step has an Android part and an iPhone part. Five short steps.</Fix>

<StepByStep>
<Step>
**Get voice typing ready.**

**Android:** Install Gboard from the Play Store. Google says some steps work only on Android 7.0 and up.
{/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S1 relay; the page for making Gboard your default keyboard was not read) */}

**iPhone:** Go to Settings, then General, then Keyboard. Turn on Enable Dictation. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S3 is a search relay; confirm the menu path on Apple's page) */}
</Step>
<Step>
**Tap a text field.**

**Android:** Open an app where you can type, such as Gmail or Keep. Tap the text field.
{/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S1 relay) */}

**iPhone:** Open any app where you can type. Tap the text field.
</Step>
<Step>
**Tap the microphone.**

**Android:** Tap the Microphone icon at the top of the keyboard. If your phone asks to use the microphone, you will see "While using app", "Only this time" and "Don't allow". Choose the one you are comfortable with.
{/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S1 relay) */}

**iPhone:** Tap the Dictate button on the onscreen keyboard. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S3 is a search relay) */}
</Step>
<Step>
**Speak, and say the punctuation.**

**Android:** When you see "Speak now", say what you want written. Say punctuation as a word: Period, Comma, Exclamation point, Question mark, New line or New paragraph.
{/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S1 relay) */}

**iPhone:** Speak your message. Basic punctuation words work here too. You can also try Settings, then General, then Keyboard, then Auto-Punctuation. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S3 and S4 are search relays) */}
</Step>
<Step>
**Fix one word, then stop.**

**Android:** Touch and hold the word you want to change. Tap the Microphone. When you see "Speak now", say the new word.
{/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S1 relay) */}

**iPhone:** To stop, tap Dictate, say "Stop dictation", or stay silent for 30 seconds. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S3 is a search relay) */}
</Step>
</StepByStep>

<Tip>Gboard is also on iPhone. Google lists the same steps as Android, and says Gboard needs iOS 9 or later on iPhone and iPad. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S2 relay) */}</Tip>

<Tip>On iPhone, Apple says Dictation is processed on your iPhone in many languages, and it may not be available in all languages or regions. If you dictate into a search field, the text may be sent to the search engine. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S3 is a search relay) */}</Tip>

<Warning>Always read dictated text before you send it, and fix any word that came out wrong. Do not send a message you have not read.</Warning>

<Safety>Do not dictate passwords, card numbers or private details where other people can hear you. In Gboard, Settings, then Privacy has an Audio donations switch, which is voluntary. Google says human reviewers may listen to or transcribe some donated snippets. The same page has "Delete learned words & data". We did not find a statement on how standard Gboard voice typing handles your audio, so we make no claim either way. {/* CHECK: re-read on live page (S5 relay) */}</Safety>
