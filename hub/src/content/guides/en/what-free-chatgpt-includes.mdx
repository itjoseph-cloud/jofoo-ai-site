<TLDR>The free ChatGPT plan costs nothing and has no set time limit. It includes core features such as web search, file and image questions, study mode and image creation, with usage limits. OpenAI publishes no numbers for those limits, as of 2026-11-05.</TLDR>

<Problem>You hear that ChatGPT is free. Then the questions start. Can you upload a file? Can you make images? Will it stop in the middle of a task? You want to know before you rely on it.</Problem>

<Why>
Free has no set time limit. OpenAI's Free page says it includes core features with usage limits. The limits are the part to understand.

The pricing page marks several features as Limited. These are uploads, image creation, voice, deep research, and memory and context. It also marks Codex and ChatGPT Work on desktop as Limited. It gives no numbers, so you cannot know in advance when you will reach a limit. {/* CHECK: S1 is a reader quote, spot-check the Limited list on chatgpt.com/pricing before publishing. */}

The same card describes text chats as "unlimited", with a note that abuse guardrails apply. {/* CHECK: quoted from the pricing card, confirm exact wording on the day. */}

Paid plans sit above Free. They are Go, Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise. This guide gives no prices for them.

Two more things can surprise you. Ads may appear on Free in some regions. And data sharing for model training is on by default.
</Why>

<Fix>Check five things in order: your plan, the features, the limits, ads, and your data settings. Use made-up input while you explore.</Fix>

<StepByStep>
<Step>**Open ChatGPT and sign in.** {/* CHECK: F1 is a reader paraphrase of chatgpt.com/plans/free, confirm wording. */} Go to chatgpt.com, then create an account or log in. OpenAI's Free page says the plan is for anyone who creates an account.</Step>
<Step>**Find your plan name.** Your account shows which plan you are on. {/* CHECK: record where the plan name appears in the demo. */} You do not need to select anything labelled upgrade to follow this guide.</Step>
<Step>**Try each feature on made-up input.** Use the sample prompts below for web search, a file upload, study mode and image creation. {/* CHECK: record where each feature sits on screen in the demo. */} OpenAI's Free page lists all four. {/* CHECK: upload and study mode wording are marked "recheck" in RESEARCH F5/F6. */} The Free page states no limit for image creation, but the pricing page lists it as Limited.</Step>
<Step>**Notice how a limit shows up.** If you reach one, read the message and write down its exact words. {/* CHECK: record the real limit message wording in the demo. */} OpenAI publishes no numbers for limits, as of 2026-11-05. Re-check the pricing page if a feature stops working.</Step>
<Step>**Check whether ads appear.** OpenAI says ads may appear for users on the Free and Go plans, in select regions. {/* CHECK: ads rollout is region by region, confirm the help article still says "select regions". */} It also says ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers. You can choose an ad-free option, but it has a trade-off. Go to Settings, then Ads controls, then Change plan to go ad-free. OpenAI says this means reduced message limits, lower usage limits and reduced feature access. It is your choice.</Step>
<Step>**Open your data controls.** OpenAI says that on personal workspaces with ChatGPT Free, data sharing is enabled by default. To change it on the web, open the account menu, then Settings, then Data controls. Find "Improve the model for everyone", turn it off, and select Done. In the app, open the sidebar, tap your profile icon, then Data controls, and turn it off there.</Step>
<Step>**Know what that switch does and does not do.** Turning it off does not delete or hide your saved chats. Voice mode has a separate control for sharing data for model training. Check that control too.</Step>
<Step>**Use Temporary chat for one-off questions.** OpenAI says Temporary chats are not in your history, use no memories, and are not used to improve its models. They may be kept for up to 30 days for safety purposes. To start one, open a new chat and select Temporary. OpenAI also says ads do not appear in Temporary chats.</Step>
<Step>**Re-check the plan page on the day you use it.** Plans change. Open [chatgpt.com/pricing](https://chatgpt.com/pricing) and read the Free card again before you rely on a feature.</Step>
</StepByStep>

<Warning>Free has usage limits, and OpenAI publishes no numbers for them. If a task has a deadline, do not assume Free will carry you through to the end.</Warning>

<Safety>On Free, data sharing for model training is on by default. Turning it off helps, but it does not cover everything. OpenAI says that if you give a chat a thumbs up or thumbs down, the entire conversation tied to that feedback may be used to train its models. Keep passwords, ID numbers, health details and client information out of your chats.</Safety>

<Tip>Signed-in Free users can request a copy of their data and can delete their account from settings, according to OpenAI. {/* CHECK: GPT source URL uses the article ID 8554407 only, confirm the full slug and that the page still says this. */} You can also use GPTs on Free when you are signed in. New GPT creation and publishing are not available on personal accounts, including Free.</Tip>
